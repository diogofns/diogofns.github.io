About

I'm Diogo Fernandes, a Senior Researcher at CEIA-UFG and a PhD student in Computer Science at UFG.

I work on Natural Language Processing (NLP), with a focus on efficient Large Language Models (LLMs). My research includes LLM Pretraining and Post-training, knowledge distillation, and agentic AI.

Projects

Lumi-4B

CEIA-UFG's small language model for Brazilian Portuguese, designed to make text generation more efficient and affordable.
