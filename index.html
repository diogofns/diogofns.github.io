About

I'm Diogo Fernandes, a Senior Researcher at CEIA-UFG and a PhD student in Computer Science at UFG.

I work on Natural Language Processing (NLP), with a focus on efficient Large Language Models (LLMs). My research includes LLM Pretraining and Post-training, knowledge distillation, and agentic AI.

Projects

Lumi-4B
CEIA-UFG · 2026

CEIA-UFG's small language model for Brazilian Portuguese, designed to make text generation more efficient and affordable.

ECHO
EACL 2027

An activation steering framework for controlling LLM personas, complementing system prompts and improving conversational expression.

ReSuME
AACL-IJCNLP 2026

An agent memory method that uses sparse autoencoders to select which dialogue turns to retain, improving retrieval on MemoryAgentBench.

ParaSpeechRAG
EMNLP 2026

A benchmark for speech retrieval under changes in speaker, prosody, and acoustic conditions, revealing how these variations affect retrieval performance.

SC-LoRA
CoLoRAI @ ICML 2026

A LoRA method that uses sparse autoencoder features to select specialist adapters and allocate rank per token, reducing forgetting across tasks.

The Selective Safety Trap
ICBINB @ ICLR 2026 · Didactic Paper Award

A bilingual evaluation of LLM safety across minority groups, exposing differences in defense rates under matched adversarial attacks.
